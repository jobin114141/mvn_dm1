Ras Al Khaimah’s Leading Offshore Jurisdiction.

Ras al-Khaimah is one of the most popular investment destinations in the UAE, known for its excellent connectivity, ease of doing business, and lucrative tax benefits. RAK International Corporate Centre (RAK ICC) was formed under Decree No.12 of 2015, consolidating RAK IC and RAK Offshore into a single, powerful offshore authority.

✦ KEY CHARACTERISTICS

RAK ICC Setup – What You Need to Know.

Key structural and regulatory requirements for Ras al-Khaimah offshore company formation before you proceed.

Shareholders

At least one shareholder required. Corporate shareholders are acceptable.

Directors

At least one director mandatory. Need not be UAE resident or national. Corporate directors acceptable.

Share Capital

Fully paid-up share capital of AED 1,000 minimum requirement.

Formation Timeline

Approximately 2-3 working days, subject to name approval by RAK ICC.

Local Office

A registered office in RAK ICC Offshore provided by a licensed service provider is mandatory.

Business Activities

Wide range: Trading, IT, Media, Consulting, Logistics, Engineering, Manufacturing, Healthcare, and more.

✦ DOCUMENTS REQUIRED

Documents for RAK ICC Formation.

Required from shareholders, directors, and secretary. The shareholder, director, and secretary may be the same person.

✦Desired offshore company name (3 options)
✦Information about shares held by each shareholder
✦Notarized & attested board resolution, Power of Attorney, MOA & AOA, and Certificate of Incorporation (for corporate shareholders)
✦Original bank reference letter
✦Original latest utility bill & original residence proof
✦Passport copies (minimum 3 months validity)
✦Resume or professional profile
✦Valid UAE Visa or Entry Stamp page copy
LIMITATIONS TO NOTE
Cannot attain a Tax Residence Certificate (TRC) in UAE
Cannot conduct business within UAE
Cannot issue UAE residence visas
Cannot rent physical office space in UAE
