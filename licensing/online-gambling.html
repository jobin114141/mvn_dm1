✦ TECHNICAL ROADMAP

iGaming Technical & RNG Compliance Flow

Step-by-step technical auditing process to pass gaming authority inspections and earn operational clearance.

STAGE 01

Platform Infrastructure

Establish secure cloud hosting, redundant backup servers, and geo-blocking firewall configurations.

STAGE 02

RNG Lab Audit

Submit game math and Random Number Generator source code to accredited testing labs (GLI / BMM / iTech).

STAGE 03

Responsible Gaming (RG)

Integrate deposit limits, session timers, self-exclusion triggers, and reality-check notification popups.

STAGE 04

Segregated Custody Vault

Open ring-fenced bank or merchant accounts ensuring player funds are separated from operational capital.

STAGE 05

Authority Sign-Off

Final compliance audit clearance, regulatory fee settlement, and issuance of operational gaming license.
