✦ FIDUCIARY ARCHITECTURE

What Does Trust Formation Involve?

BOOK A CONSULTATION ↗

A trust is a powerful legal relationship under Common Law through which an individual or corporate entity (the Settlor) transfers legal title of assets to a trusted fiduciary (the Trustee) to hold, manage, and distribute for the benefit of designated beneficiaries.

Trust structures provide total separation of legal ownership from beneficial entitlement, insulating family wealth, operating equity, real estate, and investment portfolios from forced heirship claims, external commercial liabilities, and probate delays.

Maven Consults guides high-net-worth families, enterprise owners, and institutional investors in structuring robust, compliant trust arrangements across DIFC, ADGM, and premier international trust jurisdictions.

✦ STRUCTURING OPTIONS

Specialised Trust Categories

Select a trust model to review its governing framework, protection tier, and primary strategic applications.

CATEGORY 01

Discretionary Family Trust

Grants trustee discretion over timing and proportion of beneficiary distributions, offering maximum flexibility across generations.

  • ✦ Shielding against beneficiary marriage breakdown
  • ✦ Protection against future personal creditor claims
  • ✦ Adaptable to changing family financial needs
CATEGORY 02

Private Trust Company (PTC)

A dedicated corporate entity acting as trustee for family trusts, allowing family members to sit on the board of directors.

  • ✦ Total family governance control without institutional trustees
  • ✦ Confidential administration of family business shares
  • ✦ Seamless long-term succession framework
CATEGORY 03

Employee Benefit & EOP Trust

Corporate trust holding shares or pension reserves for key executives, employee stock ownership plans (ESOP), and deferred bonuses.

  • ✦ Institutional ring-fencing of employee equity plans
  • ✦ Vesting schedule management & payout governance
  • ✦ Tax-efficient corporate structuring for international staff
