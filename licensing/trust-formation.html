✦ FIDUCIARY ARCHITECTURE

What Does Trust Formation Involve?

BOOK A CONSULTATION ↗

A trust is a powerful legal relationship under Common Law through which an individual or corporate entity (the Settlor) transfers legal title of assets to a trusted fiduciary (the Trustee) to hold, manage, and distribute for the benefit of designated beneficiaries.

Trust structures provide total separation of legal ownership from beneficial entitlement, insulating family wealth, operating equity, real estate, and investment portfolios from forced heirship claims, external commercial liabilities, and probate delays.

Maven Consults guides high-net-worth families, enterprise owners, and institutional investors in structuring robust, compliant trust arrangements across DIFC, ADGM, and premier international trust jurisdictions.

✦ FAQ

Trust Formation Questions

Ask Maven directly →
What assets can be settled into an international trust? +
A settlor can transfer operating company shares, commercial and residential real estate, liquid investment portfolios, intellectual property, and luxury assets into a trust structure.
What is a Private Trust Company (PTC)? +
A PTC is a corporate entity established solely to act as trustee for specific family trusts. It allows family members and trusted advisors to serve as directors on the board, retaining governance oversight over trust assets.
Can a trust protect assets against creditor claims? +
Irrevocable trusts settled cleanly prior to any claim or liability transfer legal ownership away from the settlor, creating a legal barrier against future commercial liabilities under Common Law.
What is the role of the Protector in a trust? +
The Protector acts as a supervisor appointed by the Settlor holding veto rights over key trustee actions, such as appointing/removing trustees or approving major capital distributions.
Are trusts registered on public registries? +
No. Trust deeds in major Common Law jurisdictions remain private legal contracts between the settlor and trustee, ensuring high levels of beneficial confidentiality.
