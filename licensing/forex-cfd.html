✦ SERVICE OVERVIEW

What Does Forex & CFD Licensing Involve?

BOOK A CONSULTATION ↗

Forex and CFD businesses operate within regulated financial services environments. Establishing such a business involves understanding the applicable regulatory framework, identifying an appropriate jurisdiction and structuring the company in a way that meets the requirements of the relevant authority.

Licensing requirements for Forex and CFD operations can include minimum capital obligations, compliance infrastructure, qualified personnel requirements, operational standards and ongoing regulatory reporting. These requirements vary between jurisdictions.

Maven Consults helps businesses understand the licensing landscape for their specific operating model, evaluate available jurisdictions and navigate the establishment process with greater clarity.

✦ OPERATIONAL BROKERAGE MODELS

Forex & CFD Execution Structuring

MODEL A

STP / ECN (A-Book)

Direct pass-through of client orders to liquidity providers. Lower capital risk profile and streamlined regulatory requirements.

MODEL B

Market Maker (B-Book)

Broker acts as counterparty to client trades. Requires higher capital reserves and enhanced risk management oversight.

MODEL C

Hybrid Execution

Combines A-Book and B-Book routing based on volume and risk parameters. Requires comprehensive risk policy approval.

MODEL D

Introducing Broker (IB)

Referral and client onboarding entity working with licensed primary brokers. Streamlined licensing or registration framework.

✦ REQUIREMENTS

What You May Need to Prepare

Requirements vary depending on the business activity, jurisdiction and regulatory framework. Maven Consults will advise on the specific requirements applicable to your situation.

For Forex and CFD licensing, regulatory authorities typically require applicants to provide detailed information relating to the business and its principals. Common areas of review include:

  • ✦ Business model, services offered and client base
  • ✦ Ownership structure and beneficial ownership information
  • ✦ Director qualifications and professional background
  • ✦ Business plan and financial projections
  • ✦ Source of funds and capital information
  • ✦ Compliance policies and internal control frameworks
  • ✦ Identification and verification documents
  • ✦ Technology and platform arrangements

✦ FAQ

Forex & CFD Licensing Questions

Ask Maven directly →
What types of Forex or CFD businesses require licensing? +
Businesses offering foreign exchange trading, CFD products or related investment services to clients typically require regulatory authorisation in the jurisdiction in which they operate. The specific requirements depend on the business model and services offered.
Which jurisdiction is most suitable for a Forex or CFD business? +
The most suitable jurisdiction depends on your business model, target client base, capital position and operational requirements. Maven can help you evaluate the available options based on your specific situation.
Are there capital requirements for Forex and CFD licensing? +
Yes. Most jurisdictions impose minimum capital requirements on licensed Forex and CFD businesses. These requirements vary between jurisdictions and depend on the specific licence category and business activities.
Can Maven help with compliance after the licence is obtained? +
Where applicable, Maven can support ongoing compliance obligations, including regulatory reporting, licence renewals and related corporate services after the business has been established.
How does Maven differ from a law firm or regulatory consultant? +
Maven Consults is a business consultancy focused on helping clients understand their options, structure their business and navigate the establishment process. Where specific legal advice is required, Maven can refer clients to appropriate specialists.
