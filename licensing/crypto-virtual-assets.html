✦ SERVICE OVERVIEW

What Does Crypto & Virtual Asset Licensing Involve?

BOOK A CONSULTATION ↗

The virtual asset sector continues to evolve rapidly. Regulatory frameworks for crypto and digital asset businesses have developed significantly across multiple jurisdictions, with dedicated licensing regimes now established in many markets.

Virtual Asset Service Providers (VASPs) — including those offering exchange, custody, transfer, administration or advisory services related to virtual assets — typically require specific regulatory authorisation. The applicable framework depends on the nature of the services provided, the operating model and the chosen jurisdiction.

Maven Consults helps businesses in the digital asset sector understand the licensing landscape, evaluate suitable jurisdictions and navigate the establishment process with a structured approach.

What You May Need to Prepare

Requirements vary depending on the business activity, jurisdiction and regulatory framework. Maven Consults will advise on the specific requirements applicable to your situation.

Virtual asset licensing applications commonly require businesses to provide detailed information about their services, technical infrastructure and compliance arrangements. Typical areas of review include:

✦ FAQ

Crypto & Virtual Asset Licensing Questions

Ask Maven directly →
What is a VASP and does my business qualify? +
A Virtual Asset Service Provider (VASP) is a business that offers exchange, transfer, custody, issuance or related services involving virtual assets. Whether your business qualifies depends on the services it provides and the jurisdiction's definition. Maven can help you evaluate this.
Which jurisdiction has the most developed virtual asset framework? +
Several jurisdictions have developed dedicated virtual asset regulatory frameworks. The most suitable jurisdiction depends on your business model, target market and operational requirements. Maven can help you evaluate the available options.
Are AML/CFT requirements significant for virtual asset businesses? +
Yes. Virtual asset businesses are typically subject to significant AML/CFT obligations. Most jurisdictions require VASPs to implement robust compliance programmes, customer due diligence and transaction monitoring as part of the licensing requirements.
Can a startup apply for virtual asset licensing? +
Yes. New businesses can apply for virtual asset licensing, subject to meeting the regulatory requirements of the chosen jurisdiction. Understanding these requirements early helps ensure the business is structured appropriately from the outset.
Can Maven help after the licence has been obtained? +
Where applicable, Maven can continue to support regulatory reporting, licence renewals and corporate compliance requirements after the business has been established and licensed.
