What Does Payments & Fintech Licensing Involve?

BOOK A CONSULTATION ↗

Payment service providers, money transfer operators and fintech platforms operate within tightly regulated financial environments. Establishing a payment or fintech business requires careful navigation of licensing frameworks, safeguarding rules and banking arrangements.

Depending on the business model, licensing requirements can cover e-money issuance, merchant acquiring, payment initiation, account information services or cross-border remittance. Each activity carries specific regulatory standards.

Maven Consults helps fintech founders and payment operators evaluate suitable jurisdictions, structure their business model and navigate the licensing journey.

✦ FINTECH LICENSING SPECTRUM

Payment Activities & Regulatory Frameworks

SPECTRUM 01

Payment Gateway (PSP)

Technical processing, merchant acquiring and transaction gateway authorisation without holding customer balances.

SPECTRUM 02

Electronic Money (EMI)

Issuance of e-money, digital wallets and stored value accounts requiring strict central bank safeguarding.

SPECTRUM 03

Remittance & FX

Cross-border money transfer and foreign currency exchange services with correspondent banking requirements.

SPECTRUM 04

Open Banking / API

Account Initiation (PISP) and Account Information (AISP) aggregated services under modern PSD2 standards.
