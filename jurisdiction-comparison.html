UAE Offshore Jurisdiction Comparison:
JAFZA vs. RAK ICC vs. Ajman Offshore

Choosing the right offshore jurisdiction matters. Here’s how the UAE’s three main offshore options compare across key parameters.

BY OFFSHORE ADVISORY DESK  ·  6 MIN READ  ·  AUGUST 2026
The United Arab Emirates offers three prominent non-resident offshore jurisdictions—JAFZA Offshore in Dubai, RAK ICC in Ras Al Khaimah, and Ajman Offshore. While all three provide legal corporate veil protection, zero corporate tax, and 100% foreign ownership, strategic differences in banking reputation, corporate governance, real estate ownership rights, and operational costs make each uniquely suited for distinct business objectives.

In-Depth Jurisdiction Analysis

DUBAI

JAFZA Offshore

Established in 2003 under the Jebel Ali Free Zone Authority, JAFZA Offshore is the premier offshore structure in Dubai. It holds the highest tier of global recognition among international financial institutions.

  • Only offshore structure permitted to hold Dubai real estate directly
  • Unmatched global prestige & corporate banking acceptance
  • No minimum share capital requirement
  • Ideal for holding company structures, IP rights & logistics
RAS AL KHAIMAH

RAK ICC Offshore

Consolidated in 2015, Ras Al Khaimah International Corporate Centre (RAK ICC) offers a modern, highly flexible corporate framework governed by common-law principles.

  • Allows corporate bodies to act as directors
  • Fast 2–3 day incorporation turnaround
  • Strong common-law legal foundation with DIFC/ADGM court access
  • Cost-efficient for general international holding & asset protection
AJMAN

Ajman Offshore

Introduced in 2014 by the Ajman Free Zone Authority, Ajman Offshore provides a fast-track, highly economical jurisdiction designed for budget-conscious international entrepreneurs.

  • Most budget-friendly incorporation & renewal cost structure
  • Requires a minimum of 2 directors for enhanced corporate checks
  • Permits ownership of up to 3 real estate properties
  • DTAA Treaty access spanning 47 countries

How to Choose the Right Offshore Jurisdiction

Selecting the correct offshore jurisdiction depends heavily on your intended business activities, banking requirements, asset locations, and governance preferences:

Strategic Guidance:

Choose JAFZA Offshore if you plan to acquire property in Dubai, require maximum prestige for international bank account openings, or are structuring major intellectual property and global trading operations.

Choose RAK ICC if you require corporate directors, desire a flexible English common-law environment, or wish to structure holding entities with seamless re-domiciliation mechanisms.

Choose Ajman Offshore if setup velocity and minimal annual operational maintenance costs are your top priorities for straightforward holding or global consulting activities.
