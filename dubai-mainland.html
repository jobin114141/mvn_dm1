✦ WHAT IS DUBAI MAINLAND?

Operate Across the UAE Without Restriction

BOOK A CONSULTATION ↗

A Dubai Mainland company is a business entity licensed by the Dubai Department of Economy and Tourism (DET), formerly known as the Department of Economic Development (DED). Unlike free zone companies — which are restricted to operating within their designated zones — a mainland company can trade directly with the UAE market, engage government entities, and operate from any location across the Emirates.

Following regulatory amendments introduced in 2021, foreign investors can now hold up to 100% ownership in most mainland business activities, removing the previous requirement for a local UAE national sponsor in the majority of commercial sectors.

Maven Consults advises on the appropriate company structure, applicable licence category, and end-to-end formation requirements for businesses exploring Dubai Mainland as their preferred jurisdiction.

No restriction on UAE market activity. Trade locally, hire freely, grow without geographic limits.
✦ LICENCE & STRUCTURE TYPES

Dubai Mainland Company Structures & Licences.

The DET issues several categories of business licence across a range of legal structures. Maven can help you identify the most appropriate option for your activity and ownership requirements.

STRUCTURE 01

Limited Liability Company (LLC)

The most common structure for foreign investors in Dubai Mainland. Requires a minimum of two shareholders. Up to 100% foreign ownership is now permitted in most commercial activities, removing the need for a local UAE partner in the majority of sectors.

STRUCTURE 02

Branch Office

An extension of a foreign parent company into the Dubai market. The branch carries out activities identical to the parent and is 100% owned by the foreign entity. A local service agent is required for administrative registration purposes.

STRUCTURE 03

Civil Company

Suited for professional service providers including legal, medical, engineering and accounting firms. Partners in a civil company hold joint liability. This structure enables licensed professionals to practice under the Dubai Mainland framework.

STRUCTURE 04

Sole Establishment

A single-owner structure where the individual bears full liability. Available to UAE and GCC nationals, and to certain professional categories. Suitable for small-scale professional or commercial operations under a single individual's ownership.

STRUCTURE 05

Representative Office

A non-commercial presence used to promote the parent company's products or services in the UAE without conducting direct commercial activity. Suitable for foreign companies wishing to build a market presence before full incorporation.

STRUCTURE 06

Joint Stock Company (PJSC / PJSC)

For larger enterprises seeking capital investment structures or public listing. Both private and public joint stock company frameworks are available under UAE commercial company law for qualifying businesses.

✦ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Dubai Mainland Formation FAQs

Can a foreigner own 100% of a Dubai Mainland company? +
Yes. Following amendments to UAE Commercial Companies Law introduced in 2021, foreign investors can now hold up to 100% ownership in most commercial and professional activities on the Dubai Mainland, without the need for a UAE national sponsor or partner. Certain strategic or restricted activities may still require specific ownership arrangements — Maven can advise based on your intended activity.
What is the difference between a Dubai Mainland company and a free zone company? +
A Dubai Mainland company is licensed by the DET and can trade freely across the UAE domestic market, including with local businesses, consumers and government entities. A free zone company operates within a designated economic zone, which typically restricts direct trading within the UAE mainland without the use of a local distributor or agent. Mainland companies are generally more suitable for businesses requiring direct local market access, retail presence or government contracts.
Do I need a physical office for a Dubai Mainland company? +
Yes. A physical office address within the Dubai Mainland is generally required for DET licence issuance. The size and type of office required may vary depending on the business activity and the number of visas you intend to apply for. Maven can advise on suitable office arrangements as part of the formation process.
How long does it take to form a Dubai Mainland company? +
Timelines vary depending on the activity, structure and completeness of documentation. In straightforward cases, a DET commercial licence can be issued within a few working days once all documents are in order. More complex activities or structures may take longer. Maven Consults manages the formation process to ensure accuracy and efficiency throughout.
Can a mainland company have branches in the UAE? +
Yes. A Dubai Mainland company can open branches across Dubai and other UAE emirates. Each branch is registered under the parent trade licence and can operate across the UAE domestic market. Maven can advise on branch registration requirements as your business expands.
How do I get started with Dubai Mainland formation? +
Begin by speaking with Maven about your intended business activity, ownership structure and operational requirements. Our team will review your objectives, confirm the appropriate mainland structure and licence category, and guide you through every step of the formation process.
