ENTITY STRUCTURE OPTIONS

FZE vs FZC Corporate Models

Sharjah free zone company registration allows two primary shareholding structures tailored around investor requirements:

FZE (Free Zone Establishment) Single shareholder (individual or corporate entity) with limited liability.
FZC (Free Zone Company) 2 to 5 shareholders operating as a multi-partner limited liability enterprise.
