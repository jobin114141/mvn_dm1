✦ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Compliance Services — Common Questions

What are compliance services?+
Compliance services help businesses understand, organise and manage relevant corporate, regulatory, documentation and reporting requirements applicable to their business structure and activities.
Do I need compliance support after setting up my company?+
Company formation is only the beginning. Businesses may have ongoing corporate and regulatory responsibilities after establishment, depending on their structure, activities and applicable jurisdiction.
What does Maven's compliance service cover?+
Maven's compliance offering can include corporate compliance, regulatory compliance, AML compliance, UBO requirements, ESR where applicable, corporate documentation, annual compliance and regulatory reporting.
Does every business have the same compliance requirements?+
No. Requirements can vary depending on the business structure, activities and applicable jurisdiction. Maven can help identify the requirements relevant to your specific business situation.
Can Maven help with ongoing compliance?+
Yes. Maven's compliance services are designed to support businesses with relevant ongoing and recurring requirements, not just initial setup tasks.
Does Maven provide AML compliance support?+
AML compliance support is included within the compliance service offering where applicable to the business and its activities.
What are UBO requirements?+
UBO requirements relate to information concerning the Ultimate Beneficial Owner of a business. The specific requirements depend on the applicable business structure and jurisdiction.
Is ESR applicable to every business?+
No. Economic Substance Requirements may apply depending on the relevant business activities and circumstances. Maven can help determine whether ESR applies based on the business situation.
