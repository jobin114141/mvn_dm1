✦ GCC CUSTOMS TARIFF EXEMPTION PROTOCOL

Duty-Free Export Access Across All AGCC Member States.

Under Ajman Free Zone’s specialized National Industrial License framework, manufacturing entities established within AFZ enjoy 0% customs duty trade across Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Bahrain, Oman, Qatar, and the UAE.

To qualify for this strategic AGCC tariff exemption, companies must satisfy two statutory eligibility benchmarks enforced by AFZA:

1. 51%+ GCC Equity Shareholding The entity must be registered with GCC nationals or GCC corporate entities holding a majority shareholding (51% or above).
2. Minimum 40% In-Zone Value Addition A minimum of 40% of the manufacturing and value-addition process must be accomplished within Ajman Free Zone facilities.
