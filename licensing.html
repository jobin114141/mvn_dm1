✦ FAQ

Licensing Questions, Answered

Have a question not listed here? Contact Maven directly.

Which licence does my business need? +
The appropriate licence depends on your business activity, operating model, jurisdiction and applicable regulatory requirements. Maven can help you understand the available options and identify the most appropriate route for your business.
Can Maven help us choose a jurisdiction? +
Yes. Jurisdiction selection is an important part of specialised licensing. Maven can help you evaluate available options based on your business requirements, including the regulatory environment, ongoing obligations and structural considerations of each jurisdiction.
Do licensing requirements differ between jurisdictions? +
Yes. Licensing frameworks, requirements and ongoing obligations can vary significantly between jurisdictions. What is required in one jurisdiction may differ substantially from another, even for similar business activities.
Can an existing business apply for specialised licensing? +
Existing businesses may explore licensing options when expanding activities, entering new markets or establishing operations in another jurisdiction. The appropriate route depends on the specific circumstances of the business.
How long does the licensing process take? +
The timeline depends on the business activity, jurisdiction, regulatory requirements and the completeness of the required documentation. Maven can give you a clearer indication once your specific situation has been reviewed.
Can Maven support us after licensing? +
Where applicable, Maven can also support businesses with related corporate services, accounting, VAT, visas and compliance requirements after the licence has been obtained.
