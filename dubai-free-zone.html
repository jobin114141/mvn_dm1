✦ CORPORATE STRUCTURES

Types of Companies Registered in Dubai Free Zones.

There are predefined legal requirements and guidelines which need to be fulfilled to register a business entity in any free zone. Maven takes into consideration short & long term goals, investment capital, and legal structure before suggesting the ideal entity type.

SINGLE SHAREHOLDER ENTITY

Free Zone Establishment (FZE)

A single-shareholder establishment formed with limited liability. The shareholder can be either an individual natural person, a corporate entity, or an established company.

Best For: Solo entrepreneurs, single corporate parent holdings, and independent investors.
MULTIPLE SHAREHOLDER ENTITY

Free Zone Company (FZCO)

Requires 2 or more shareholders to be registered. Shareholders can be a combination of individual persons, corporate entities, or international companies.

Best For: Partnerships, joint ventures, multi-investor commercial companies, and group subsidiaries.
FOREIGN EXTENSION

Branch or Representative Office

An extension of an existing foreign or UAE parent company, retaining the same legal name and trading activity scope as its parent entity without separate share capital requirement.

Best For: Established global corporations expanding regional operations into Dubai.
✦ LICENSING CATEGORIES

Business Licenses Available in Dubai Free Zones.

Depending on your commercial activity type and operational requirements, Maven assists in securing official authority authorization for any of the following 5 core license categories.

General Trading License

Allows companies to trade in multiple un-related general goods and merchandise under a single unified permit.

Trading / Commercial License

Permits specified buying, selling, import, export, storage, and distribution of defined goods and commodities.

Service License

Designed for companies providing specific specialized services such as accounting, advisory, IT & marketing.

Professional License

Issued to qualified individuals or firms providing personal professional services, HR consulting, or PR consultancy.

Industrial License

Granted to entities carrying out manufacturing, raw material processing, assembly, or product packaging activities.
