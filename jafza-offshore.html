✦ JAFZA OFFSHORE — ESTABLISHED 2003

Dubai’s Original Offshore Framework.

Realizing the strategic importance of attracting foreign investment, the Jebel Ali Free Zone Authority (JAFZA) — in partnership with Dubai’s progressive Government — introduced the JAFZA Offshore Companies Regulations on January 13, 2003. This made Dubai one of the world’s first Gulf states to offer a compliant, fully regulated offshore jurisdiction.

The JAFZA offshore framework gives international businesses access to the UAE’s Double Taxation Avoidance Agreements (DTAA) with over 40 countries, while delivering operational transparency and sustainable development in a tax-neutral environment. ✦ FORMATION REQUIREMENTS

Setting Up a JAFZA Offshore Company.

JAFZA maintains clear and transparent formation requirements. Here are the key structural parameters for an offshore company under JAFZA Regulations 2003.

01
ShareholdersMinimum one shareholder required. Corporate shareholders are permitted. Nominee shareholders are also acceptable.
02
DirectorsMinimum one director required. No UAE residency required. Corporate directors are NOT permitted under JAFZA regulations — directors must be natural persons.
03
Share CapitalNo minimum share capital requirement. Only one share is needed. Capital can be in any major international currency.
04
Registered OfficeA registered office address within JAFZA provided by a licensed registered agent is mandatory. Maven Consults provides this as part of our service.
05
Formation TimelineTypically 3–5 working days from document submission, subject to JAFZA authority name approval. No physical presence required.
06
Company NameMust end with “Offshore”. Name must not resemble existing companies or regulatory bodies. Submit 2–3 preferred names for approval.
✦ DOCUMENTS REQUIRED

What You Need to Submit.

✦Desired JAFZA offshore company name (2-3 options)
✦Passport copies of all shareholders & directors (min. 6 months validity)
✦Corporate documents (for corporate shareholders): notarized MOA, AOA, Certificate of Incorporation, board resolution, POA
✦Original bank reference letter
✦Proof of residence (utility bill, not older than 3 months)
✦CV/professional profile of each shareholder and director
✦UAE entry stamp or visa page copy (if applicable)
⚠ JAFZA OFFSHORE LIMITATIONS
Cannot obtain a UAE Tax Residence Certificate (TRC)
Cannot conduct direct business within the UAE market
Cannot issue UAE residence visas for shareholders
Cannot rent or lease physical office space inside the UAE
Corporate directors are not permitted (must be natural persons)
