✦ JAFZA OFFSHORE — ESTABLISHED 2003

Dubai’s Original Offshore Framework.

Realizing the strategic importance of attracting foreign investment, the Jebel Ali Free Zone Authority (JAFZA) — in partnership with Dubai’s progressive Government — introduced the JAFZA Offshore Companies Regulations on January 13, 2003. This made Dubai one of the world’s first Gulf states to offer a compliant, fully regulated offshore jurisdiction.

The JAFZA offshore framework gives international businesses access to the UAE’s Double Taxation Avoidance Agreements (DTAA) with over 40 countries, while delivering operational transparency and sustainable development in a tax-neutral environment.
