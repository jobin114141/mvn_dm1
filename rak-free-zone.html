Types of Companies Registered in RAK Free Zones.

Companies interested in RAK free zone business setup can choose between two main corporate entities:

MULTI-PARTNER ENTITY

RAK Free Zone Company

Owned by 2 to 5 partners (either individual persons or corporate shareholders) operating as a limited liability company with complete operational independence.

Ideal For: Commercial partnerships, joint ventures, and multi-investor trading companies.
DOMESTIC & FOREIGN EXTENSION

An official branch extension of an existing domestic UAE company or foreign parent company, carrying out the same business activities under the parent umbrella.

Ideal For: Established UAE onshore companies or international parent firms expanding footprint into RAK.

Business Licenses for RAK Free Zone Setup.

RAK free zone company formation requires companies to secure any of the following primary authority licenses:

RAK FTZ Commercial License

Authorizes companies to perform specified commercial trading activities, buying, selling, importing, exporting, and warehousing of goods.

RAK FTZ Industrial License

Granted to manufacturing plants, assembly lines, raw material processing, and heavy industrial packaging operations.
