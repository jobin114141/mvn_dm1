✦ STRUCTURAL PARAMETERS

Ajman Offshore Setup – Key Characteristics.

Key structural parameters and statutory requirements for an offshore company under AFZA 2014 Regulations.

Shareholders

At least one shareholder required. Corporate shareholders are fully permitted. Nominee arrangements acceptable.

Directors

Minimum two directors required. Shareholder can act as director. Corporate directors NOT permitted (must be natural persons).

Company Secretary

A director can serve as company secretary. This dual role simplifies legal filings and board administration.

Formation Timeline

Registration completes in approximately 2–3 working days once documents are signed before AFZA executives.

Registered Office

Appointment of an authorized registered agent is mandatory. Maven provides the required Ajman office address.

Permissible Scope

General Trading, Holding Companies, Consulting, Advisory, IP Ownership, Joint Ventures & International Services.

✦ DOCUMENT CHECKLIST

Required Documents for Ajman Formation.

To initiate Ajman offshore registration with AFZA, the following documentation must be submitted:

✦Passport copies of all shareholders & directors (min 6 months validity)
✦Original bank reference letter for each individual shareholder
✦Original proof of residence (utility bill or bank statement within 3 months)
✦Shareholder professional profile / CV
✦3 proposed offshore company names in order of preference
✦Valid UAE Entry Stamp or Visa Copy (if shareholder has visited UAE)
✦Share allocation breakdown between shareholders
⚠ AJMAN OFFSHORE STATUTORY LIMITATIONS
Cannot obtain a UAE Tax Residency Certificate (TRC)
Cannot conduct direct business inside the UAE mainland market
Cannot issue UAE residence visas for shareholders or employees
Cannot lease physical office space inside the UAE
Cannot own more than 3 real estate properties in approved zones
